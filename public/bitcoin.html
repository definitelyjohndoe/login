How to Buy & Send Bitcoin

A simple guide for buying BTC through Binance and sending it to another wallet.

Important: Cryptocurrency transactions can be irreversible. Always verify the receiving wallet address and network before confirming a withdrawal.
1

Log in to Binance

Open the official Binance website or app and sign in to your account.

Open Binance
2

Buy Bitcoin

Choose the option to buy cryptocurrency and select Bitcoin (BTC).

Enter the amount you want to purchase and select an available payment method.

Review the price, fees and amount of BTC you will receive before confirming the purchase.

3

Get the receiving wallet address

Open the wallet that you want to send the Bitcoin to.

Select Receive Bitcoin and copy the wallet's Bitcoin address.

Never use an address supplied by someone you don't trust. Copy the address directly from the receiving wallet and carefully check it before sending.
4

Withdraw BTC from Binance

In Binance, go to:

Assets → Overview → Withdraw

Select Bitcoin (BTC), then paste the receiving wallet address.

5

Choose the correct network

Select the blockchain network supported by the receiving wallet. For a normal Bitcoin wallet, verify that the receiving wallet specifically supports the Bitcoin network.

STOP AND CHECK: The network selected on Binance must match the network supported by the receiving wallet. Sending cryptocurrency through the wrong network can result in permanent loss of funds.
6

Enter the amount

Enter the amount of BTC you want to send.

Review the withdrawal fee and the final amount that the receiving wallet will receive.

7

Double-check everything

Before confirming, carefully check:

  • The Bitcoin wallet address
  • The selected network
  • The amount of BTC
  • The withdrawal fee
  • That you are using the correct receiving wallet
8

Confirm the withdrawal

Confirm the transaction and complete Binance's required security verification, such as two-factor authentication or email confirmation.

After Binance processes the withdrawal, you can monitor the transaction using its transaction ID.

Security reminder: Never give anyone your Binance password, two-factor authentication codes, wallet recovery phrase, or private key. Legitimate support should never require your wallet's recovery phrase.

For the latest Binance withdrawal instructions, consult Binance's official documentation before making a transaction.
